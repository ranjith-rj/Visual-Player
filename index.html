About this player

This is a browser-based IPTV player for M3U playlists. It supports HLS streams (.m3u8) and formats natively supported by your browser.

If your playlist is on a private address such as 192.168.1.7, open this page from a device that can reach that network. The IPTV server must also allow browser requests (CORS).
